The DFO January fuel energy is arithmetically consistent with its observed fuel quantity and heat content: **295 barrels × 5.8 MMBtu/barrel = 1,711 MMBtu**. Coal quantity × rounded heat content can show rounding differences, so do not force equality to a calculated value at display precision.

**Finding:** a genuine DFO fuel-consumption observation exists in EIA-923 alongside the source's DFO-associated net generation row. The lack of any DFO among EIA-860's `Energy Source 1–6` codes for the two operable Plant 6177 generator identities is therefore a **cross-form scope discrepancy**, not an indication that the DFO record was fabricated or absent.

**Do not infer:** that 160.391 MWh was independently generated by a specific generator, that all DFO was used for startup/auxiliary firing, or that the plant was short of energy. EIA-923's fuel consumption and reported net generation are plant/fuel/prime-mover observations, while EIA-860's generator energy-source codes describe generator attributes. Source-side plant, reporting BA and generator allocation are different levels of evidence.
